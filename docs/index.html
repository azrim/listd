Listd Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Listd is a task management application that integrates with Google Tasks. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we access your Google Tasks data to display and sync your tasks.

Local Data: Your task data is stored locally on your device using an encrypted SQLite database.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to display and manage your Google Tasks, sync changes between your device and Google servers.

4. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage: Your task data is stored locally on your device using an encrypted SQLite database (Drift with SQLite).

No External Servers: We do not operate our own backend servers. All data flows between your device and Google's servers.

Token Security: OAuth tokens are stored using platform-secured storage.

5. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google Tasks API and Google OAuth 2.0. See Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Your Rights

You can uninstall the app to remove all local data, or use your Google account settings to revoke Listd's access.
