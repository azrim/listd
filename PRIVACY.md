# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 2026

## 1. Introduction

Listd ("we", "our", or "us") is a task management application that integrates with Google Tasks. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Data You Provide

- **Google Account Information**: When you sign in with Google, we receive access to your Google Tasks data to display and sync your task lists and tasks.
- **Task Data**: We store your tasks and task lists locally on your device using an encrypted SQLite database.

### Automatically Collected Data

- **Local Usage Data**: We may collect basic usage statistics to improve app performance and user experience.
- **Device Information**: Basic device information (OS version, app version) for debugging purposes.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- **Provide Core Services**: Display and manage your Google Tasks, sync changes between your device and Google servers
- **Improve the App**: Understand usage patterns to enhance functionality
- **Technical Support**: Debug issues when you report problems

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage**: Your task data is stored locally on your device using an encrypted SQLite database (Drift with SQLite)
- **No External Servers**: We do not operate our own backend servers. All data flows directly between your device and Google's servers
- **Token Security**: OAuth tokens are stored using platform-secured storage (Flutter Secure Storage)

## 5. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

- **Google Tasks API**: Used to sync your tasks with Google servers. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Google OAuth 2.0**: Used for authentication. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access Your Data**: View all tasks stored locally in the app
- **Delete Local Data**: Uninstall the app to remove all local data
- **Revoke Access**: Use your Google account settings to revoke Listd's access to your Google account

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
