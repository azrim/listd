Features

Everything you need to stay on track

A thoughtfully designed set of tools to manage your daily tasks with clarity and focus.

Google Tasks Sync

Two-way sync with Google Tasks. Your data lives in your Google account — accessible everywhere, always in sync.

My Day

A focused daily view to surface what matters most today. Start each morning with intention and clarity.

Important

Star any task to surface it in your Important view. Never lose sight of what truly needs your attention.

Planned view

A timeline view of all your upcoming tasks sorted by due date. Stay ahead, never fall behind.

Dark mode

Follows your system preference automatically. Designed from the ground up for dark environments.

Secure by design

OAuth 2.0 with PKCE. No passwords stored, no data harvested. Your credentials stay in your Google account.

How it works

Up and running in minutes

Clone, sign in with Google, and your tasks are ready to go.

1

Clone the repository

Fork or clone from GitHub. Flutter prerequisites take under 5 minutes to set up.

2

Sign in with Google

OAuth 2.0 PKCE flow — no passwords, no sign-up form. Just tap and authorize.

3

Tasks sync automatically

All your Google Tasks lists appear instantly. Local changes sync back in the background.

4

Plan your day

Use My Day, Important, and Planned views to stay focused on what matters right now.
